Purchase

VxLang offers Subscription Licenses and Named Licenses (Lifetime Licenses) to accommodate different user needs.

0. Dev-Support Plan

The Dev-Support plan ($10/month) will only accept new subscriptions and re-subscriptions until November 29, 2026. Subscribers who join within this period can continue using VxLang at the existing rate of $10/month for as long as they maintain an active subscription.

After November 29, 2026, enrollment in the Dev-Support plan will be permanently closed, and the following pricing policy will apply:

  • Monthly Subscription: $30/month
  • Annual Subscription (Recommended): $180/year
    • Equivalent to just $15/month.
    • Save $180 per year (50%) compared to 12 months of monthly subscriptions.
    • Available through the annual subscription tickets described below: New User License for new users and Personal Subscription for eligible existing users.

Existing Dev-Support subscribers can retain their current subscription price. However, those who cancel their subscription will no longer be able to rejoin at the previous rate.

1. New User License

The New User License is an annual subscription ticket designed for new users who want to get started with VxLang at the best standard subscription rate.

Price: $180 USD / year — equivalent to just $15/month.

Save 50% ($180 per year) compared to paying $30/month for 12 months.

  • Includes 12 months of access to VxLang.
  • Provides access to the latest versions and updates throughout the subscription period.
  • Offers a full year of access for the cost of six months at the standard monthly rate.
  • Can be renewed through the Personal Subscription without purchasing the New User License again.

The New User License is the recommended starting point for new users choosing an annual subscription. It is a subscription license, not a Named License (Lifetime License).

Purchase New User License

2. Personal Subscription

The Personal Subscription is an annual renewal ticket for existing individual users who have previously purchased the New User License.

Price: $180 USD / year — equivalent to just $15/month.

Continue enjoying 50% savings compared to the standard monthly subscription rate.

  • Extend the validity period of your existing license by an additional 12 months.
  • Continue receiving the latest versions and updates throughout the subscription period.
  • Retain the same annual subscription rate as the New User License under the current pricing policy.
  • No need to purchase the New User License again.

The Personal Subscription is recommended for existing users who intend to continue using VxLang for the long term.

Important Notice

The Personal Subscription is available only to users who have purchased the New User License.

If you purchase a Personal Subscription without first purchasing the New User License, you will not be granted access to VxLang, and your payment will be refunded.

New users who choose an annual subscription must purchase the New User License first.

Purchase Personal Subscription

3. Named License

The Named License is a lifetime license that allows you to use VxLang indefinitely without a recurring subscription.

Named Licenses are available in two categories: Personal and Team.

3.1. Personal Named License

A lifetime license for individual developers and personal users.

  • One-time purchase.
  • No license expiration date.
  • Lifetime use without a recurring subscription.

Price: $499 USD

3.2. Team Named License

A lifetime license designed for companies, development teams, and environments where multiple developers use VxLang.

  • One-time purchase.
  • No license expiration date.
  • Team-based licensing.

Pricing and licensing terms for the Team Named License are available upon request.

Price: Contact Us

If you are interested in purchasing a Team Named License, please contact the developer directly.

5. Purchase Process

Annual Subscription License

  1. Purchase the New User License for $180 USD.
  2. Download VxLang and set up your license.
  3. Enjoy 12 months of access and updates at an equivalent rate of $15/month.
  4. Extend your license by another 12 months with a Personal Subscription ticket ($180 USD).

Named License

  1. Select either a Personal or Team Named License.
  2. Contact the developer regarding your license purchase.
  3. Receive your license.
  4. Use VxLang indefinitely.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose an annual subscription?

The standard monthly subscription costs $360 USD over 12 months. With a $180 annual subscription ticket, you receive 12 months of access for half that amount, saving $180 per year (50%). Choose the New User License if you are a new annual subscriber, or the Personal Subscription if you are eligible to renew your existing annual license.

Can new users purchase a Personal Subscription directly?

No. New users choosing an annual subscription must purchase the New User License first.

If you purchase a Personal Subscription without first purchasing the New User License, you will not be granted access to VxLang, and your payment will be refunded.

Is the New User License a lifetime license?

No. The New User License includes a one-year subscription, which can be extended through additional subscriptions.

Do I need to purchase the New User License again after one year?

No. After your initial purchase, you can purchase a Personal Subscription ticket for $180 USD to extend your license by another year.

Can I use VxLang without a recurring subscription?

Yes. By purchasing a Named License, you can use VxLang indefinitely without a recurring subscription.

Do I need a New User License to purchase a Named License?

No. Named Licenses are offered separately from Subscription Licenses.

How can I purchase a Team Named License?

Please contact the developer directly to purchase a Team Named License.
